# 📊 2025 Outlet Performance Dashboard

> 📂 **[VIEW FULL PROJECT PRESENTATION (PDF)](presentation/2025%20Outlet%20Performance%20Dashboard.pdf)**

### 🎯 Project Objectives
* **Revenue & P&L Tracking**: Monitor Net/Gross revenue and average ticket size (AOV) in real-time.
* **Customer Behavior Analysis**: Segment customers by group size (1-2, 3-4, or 4+ people) and identify peak visiting hours to optimize staffing.
* **Menu Optimization**: Identify core revenue-driving categories (Tea, Food, Cocktails) and analyze Top/Bottom performing products to refine sales strategies.

### 📈 Key Dashboard Components
The reporting system is structured into two primary views:

#### 1. Business Overview Report (General Performance)
* **Primary KPIs**: High-level tracking of Net Revenue, total invoices, and average spending per customer.
* **Revenue Distribution**: Visualizing the revenue share of each branch
* **Time-Series Trends**: Combo charts correlating revenue fluctuations with invoice counts across the months of 2025.

#### 2. Sales Detail & Product Performance
* **Category Analysis**: Identifying the product groups contributing to the majority of total revenue (e.g., Tea and Foods).
* **Product Benchmarking**: Comparative bar charts visualizing the Top 20 best-selling vs. lowest-selling items.
* **Cross-Selling Insights**: Analysis of food attachment rates based on beverage categories (Beer vs. Cocktail) to evaluate staff upselling effectiveness.

### 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Visualization**: Looker Studio.
* **Data Source**: Google Sheets.
* **Analysis Techniques**: Calculated fields for AOV, customer density mapping, and trend forecasting.
